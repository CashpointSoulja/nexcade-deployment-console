# PRD - Deployment Console

## Problem

Implementations leak time in three places: discovery drags, workflow mapping lives in documents nobody reopens, and customer data arrives late and dirty. Every stalled week is a competitor's window and a champion losing internal faith.

## Goal

Cut median signature-to-live from 23 days toward 14 (sample), keep every agent inside its guardrails with humans in control, and convert every field learning into a product change or an accelerator.

## Users

- Founding development strategist (primary operator)
- CEO (customer outcomes, board read)
- Engineering (forward-deployed scoping)
- Champions inside customer teams (their win story)

## Scope (v1 concept)

1. Implementations - every customer on one board: stage, days in stage against SLA, named blocker.
2. Quote desk - the agent's full working on a real request, from inbox to human-approved quote.
3. Agent ops - the agent fleet with volumes, guardrails and honest escalation.
4. Learnings to roadmap - heard in the field, scoped as product, shipped to core, accelerators.
5. Outcomes - signature-to-live trend, email-to-quote time, win rate, margin protected.
